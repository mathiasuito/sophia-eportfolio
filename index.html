A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Driven by curiosity.
Grounded in care.

My name is Sophia Paredes, and I am a Health Sciences student in the pre-clinical track at the University of Central Florida, graduating in December 2026.

My interest in medicine has grown through personal experiences, academic exploration, and my time working as a medical scribe in the emergency department.

Working alongside physicians and physician assistants allowed me to observe the importance of clinical knowledge, teamwork, and compassionate patient care.

As someone who speaks both English and Spanish, I value the opportunity to connect with patients through shared language and culture. My goal is to become a physician assistant who helps patients feel heard, understood, and respected.

Beyond healthcare, my involvement in leadership, community service, and professional experiences has strengthened my communication skills, responsibility, and commitment to helping others.
